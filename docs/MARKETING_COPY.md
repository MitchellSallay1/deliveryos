# Marketing copy guidelines

## Positioning

- **Product:** DeliveryOS (never “MTN DeliveryOS”)
- **Partner line:** DeliveryOS · Powered by MTN
- **Headline territory:** “The operating system for modern delivery businesses.”
- **Hero:** Deliver smarter. Operate everything from one place.

## Tone

- Enterprise, confident, minimal
- No fake statistics, customer logos, testimonials, or employee counts
- No claims that MTN SMS/USSD/MoMo are live until configured and tested

## Approved phrases

- “7-day free trial”
- “Phone sign-up” / “No credit card required for trial” (current product behavior)
- “Designed to support SMS and USSD rider operations”
- “Coming soon” for MTN integrations not yet enabled

## Legal

All public legal pages include a **draft for legal review** banner. Final copy requires counsel before production marketing launch.
